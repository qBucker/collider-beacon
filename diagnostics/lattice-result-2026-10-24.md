## Data

- 67 files, CMS Open Data ZeroBias Run2016G PFNanoAOD (CC0-1.0), pT > 20 GeV,
  |eta| < 2.5.
- Pooled n = **5,211,771** leading jets; 20,847,084 bytes of raw float32 bit
  patterns.
- The pooled count is 31 above the b = 12 round (5,211,740 decoded events):
  31 files carry an odd event count and the 12-bit packing drops one incomplete
  final symbol. The lattice arm stores raw float32 patterns and has no such
  truncation.
- Integrity: 67/67 manifests pass (file size, SHA256, finite float32, |phi| <=
  pi within one storage ULP, count plausibility, parameter record). No orphan
  files, no duplicate digests.

### Estimator details

- S1: empirical alphabet of signed float32 bit patterns, B_eff_real = 78,726;
  Clopper-Pearson at alpha = 1e-6 with Bonferroni over the fixed reference
  alphabet B_ref = 78,400. Max bin 498 counts at phi = -2.5894 rad;
  max count / n = 9.5553e-5.
- S2: mode selected on a seeded random interleave half (n = 2,605,885),
  certified on the complementary half (n = 2,605,886); selected phi = 2.0742
  rad; certify count 212; Clopper-Pearson without Bonferroni. The one-event
  asymmetry of the halves follows from the odd pooled n.

## Scope

- One dataset family (CMS Run2016G ZeroBias PFNanoAOD), pT > 20 GeV,
  |eta| < 2.5, leading-jet phi at the float32 storage precision of the public
  files.
- The result certifies a lower bound on the min-entropy of the empirical
  alphabet distribution under the stated estimator (Clopper-Pearson at
  alpha = 1e-6, Bonferroni over a fixed reference alphabet) and the
  i.i.d./stationarity model of the 2026-10-23 dependence audit. It is not a
  statement about physical entropy beyond that model.
- The lattice arm is defined by the stored representation; precision absent from
  the files cannot be recovered.
- Ceilings are zero-hypothesis synthetic values; real values sit below them by
  the physical excess.

  --require-complete --expected-files 67 --expected-n 5211771 \
  --out diagnostics/lattice-integrity.json

  --out diagnostics/lattice-alpha.json --fig diagnostics/lattice-alpha.png

The second command reads `latticeG/*.lattice.bin`. Those files (about 20 MB) are
not stored in this repository; they are regenerated with `xrd_batch_lattice.sh`
from `uris.txt`, and each manifest pins its SHA256 in `lattice_sha256`.

## Convention history

Amendments 1-2 used folded |phi|, whose storage floor is log2(pi/2^-11) = 12.65
bits. Amendment 3 adopted the signed canonical variable, which is the variable
used by the b = 8 and b = 12 measurements in the paper, with floor
log2(2*pi/2^-11) = 13.65 bits. The folded arm remains as a self-check with its
own synthetic bands. Amendments 1-4 are unchanged; their sidecars verify.
