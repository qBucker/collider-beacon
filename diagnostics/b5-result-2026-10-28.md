### P1b diagnosis (card design error)

The per-file k=7 significance has median z = 1.86, so the per-file phase is
noise-dominated and the circular resultant is diluted even for a perfectly
coherent signal. A Monte Carlo of 67 coherent phasors with signal-to-noise
|s|/sigma equal to the observed per-file z gives:

| line | per-file z (median) | E[R] under full coherence (5-95%) | measured R |
|---|---|---|---|
| k=18 | 4.32 | 0.972 (0.963-0.980) | **0.980** |
| k=7 | 1.86 | 0.821 (0.757-0.877) | **0.826** |

Both measurements sit at the centre of the full-coherence expectation. The
0.95 threshold in P1b is unreachable at this per-file SNR; the miss is a
card-design error and is not evidence against coherence. The rule recorded for
future cards: a phase-coherence threshold must be set against the per-file
signal-to-noise, not at a fixed value.

## Findings

### F1 — the 20-degree line is a fixed lab-frame feature

Pooled A18 = 0.016228 (z = 34.3), amplitude 1.62%; per-file z median 4.32 with
53/67 files above 3 sigma (C5). The cross-file phase is coherent (R = 0.980,
and the pooled amplitude equals the coherent sum of the per-file coefficients).
Pooled phase -1.5 deg; for an even harmonic this places the maxima at
multiples of 20 degrees from phi = 0.

### F2 — the modulation is carried by low-mass, two-track vertices

| stratum | n | A18 | z18 | phase (deg) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mass < 0.4 GeV | 1,163,819 | **0.0681** | **73.4** | 0.3 |
| 0.4 <= mass < 1.0 | 2,831,313 | 0.00116 | 2.0 | -136.7 |
| mass >= 1.0 GeV | 473,709 | 0.00918 | 6.3 | -179.0 |
| ntracks = 2 | 3,820,638 | 0.02003 | 39.2 | -1.5 |
| ntracks = 3 | 501,319 | 0.00450 | 3.2 | 177.7 |
| ntracks >= 4 | 146,884 | 0.01201 | 4.6 | -179.9 |
| 0 < chi2/ndof < 2 | 3,595,881 | 0.01105 | 21.0 | -5.4 |
| 2 <= chi2/ndof < 5 | 522,079 | 0.03249 | 23.5 | 3.0 |
| chi2/ndof >= 5 | 312,184 | 0.04460 | 24.9 | 3.6 |

The pooled 1.62% is the vector sum of the mass strata: 0.26 x 0.0681 with
phase 0.3 deg, minus the small anti-phase terms from the higher-mass bins,
gives 0.0162 with phase -1.5 deg — a composition closure to three digits. The
bulk of the secondary vertices (0.4-1.0 GeV, 63%) carries no modulation.

The two- and three-or-more-track strata show the same pattern: the modulation
is a two-track effect, and the multi-track residuals are small (3-5 sigma) and
anti-phase (near 180 deg). They are reported as observations, not as a second
population.

The pT strata decrease monotonically (A18 = 0.0210, 0.0167, 0.0111 for
pt < 2, 2-5, >= 5 GeV) and the fit-quality strata increase with worse fits.

### F3 — eta dependence with a fixed orientation

| eta band | n | A18 (SV) | z18 | phase | A18 (leading jet, same band) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| abs(eta) < 0.9 | 1,941,546 | 0.01967 | 27.4 | -3.5 | 0.00063 |

| 1.5-2.5 | 1,342,077 | 0.00945 | 10.9 | +7.0 | 0.00145 |

The amplitude falls by a factor 0.48 from barrel to endcap, while the phase
moves by at most 10.5 degrees. The leading-jet reference in the same bands is
consistent with zero (z <= 2), so the structure is not inherited from the jet
azimuth: the SV-minus-jet contrast is 0.019 / 0.018 / 0.008.

### F5 — the line is a smooth 18-fold modulation

A36/A18 = 0.121; the local continuum at k=36 is 0.00174, so the excess is
0.00022 (E36/A18 = 0.014). A sharp 18-sector lattice would produce an A36
comparable to A18/2; the data are consistent with a smooth modulation.

## Interpretation guard

- The k=18 line is not the seam. The seam is a localized deficit over
  +139...+150 deg with a depth of 10.5%; its Fourier content at k=18 is an
  order of magnitude below the measured line. The k=18 line is a smooth
  modulation with maxima near multiples of 20 degrees.
- The concentration in low-mass two-track vertices is consistent with a
  track-pair vertexing efficiency modulated by tracker geometry, with the
  conversion/material population (mass near zero) carrying the effect and the
  K0s-like population (0.4-1.0 GeV) showing none. This is a hypothesis. No
  geometric attribution is made in this pass.

## Limitations

- Single run period and a single product; no track-level information, so
  conversions, material interactions and combinatorial track pairs cannot be
  separated.
- The anti-phase residuals in the multi-track strata are 3-5 sigma; they are
  reported as observations.
