## Findings

### F1 — the storage floor is a branch-level product convention

Every stored azimuth value in the jet, b-tag and SV arms carries at least 11
trailing zero bits in the float32 mantissa (median exactly 11; 100.0000% of
values in all arms, 67/67 files) — 12 explicit mantissa bits. The same
7.06-bin quantization beat appears in two decoupled channels: the k=7 Fourier
line is 14.7 sigma for leading jets and 13.4 sigma for secondary vertices.
The floor is therefore a property of the product's storage convention for
angular branches, not of the jet object.

### F2 — the secondary-vertex azimuth carries high-order structure that jets lack

Fourier amplitudes at b=12:

| k | leading jet (amp, z) | SV (amp, z) |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | 0.0064, 14.7 | 0.0064, 13.4 |
| 12 | 0.0011, 2.6 | 0.0049, 10.4 |
| 17 | — | 0.0073, 15.3 |
| **18** | 0.0010, 2.0 | **0.0162, 34.3** |
| 19 | — | 0.0054, 11.5 |
| 24 | — | 0.0029, 6.0 |

Per-file stability at k=18: median z = 4.32, **53/67 files above 3 sigma**;
the leading-jet control has **0/67**. The structure is fixed across the
run period, not a statistical fluctuation, and it is not present in the jet
azimuth measured on the same events.

Interpretation guard: a localized acceptance feature produces broadband
harmonic power, so harmonic significance alone does not establish a periodic
sector lattice. Fold tests (mod 30 deg and mod 20 deg) are significant for
**both** arms and therefore do not discriminate; the differential comparison
against the jet control is the usable discriminator. The specific geometry
behind the k=18 line is not identified by this measurement.

### F3 — the dimuon arm is limited by the product content

779 events contain at least two selected muons; 579 opposite-sign leading
pairs exist in 27.1M events. Below the rule-4 threshold of 10^4 pairs, no
entropy claim is made.

The first pass computed pair masses with `E = sqrt(pT^2 + m^2)`, omitting the
longitudinal momentum; forward pairs came out with negative m^2 clipped to
zero (77% of pairs). A corrected muon-only pass (`c5_muon_count.py`,
`E = sqrt((pT cosh eta)^2 + m^2)`) gives physical masses (minimum 0.22 GeV,
median 8.7 GeV). As a descriptive check, the corrected sample shows the
J/psi peak: 81 pairs within +/-0.10 GeV of 3.096 against 5.0 per 0.10 GeV in
the sidebands; histogram peak 3.086 GeV (bin 3.076-3.096), median 3.097 GeV.
The Upsilon window holds 29 pairs with a histogram peak at 9.39 GeV. These
are content-limited checks, not entropy measurements.

## Method notes

- Fourier significance uses z = sqrt(N) * amplitude on the b=12 histogram;
  this is a Poisson-noise convention. Storage-induced correlations are present
  at the same order and are the subject of F1, not a correction to F2.
- The trailing-zero census in the first analysis version over-counted zeros
  (reported 99.98% at tz>=11 with a mode at 17-21); the per-value lowest-set-bit
  method gives 100.0000% and median 11. The corrected value is used here.
- The generation timestamp was removed from the aggregate JSON so that the
  G3 gate is literal; the run date is recorded in this note and in the commit.

## Limitations

- Single run period (2016G) and a single product (PFNanoAOD); no statement is
  made about other CMS data products.
- The SV high-order structure has no hardware model. A stratification by
  vertex quality (tracks, mass, displacement significance) requires a second
  extraction pass and is not included.
- Seam ratios are quoted with 16-bin sidebands; the 4-bin and 32-bin variants
  are in `c5-aggregate.json`.
- The dimuon arm cannot support an entropy claim at this sample size.
